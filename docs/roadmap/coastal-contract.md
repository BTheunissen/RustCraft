# Coastal implementation contract (M1-01)

Frozen 31 October 2026 after inspecting the saved survival foundation. Numeric shape tuning may be revised with measured geometry/traversal/readback evidence; public contracts must remain consistent.

## Roots, coordinates, and bounds

- One private coastal feature per existing marine catchment, derived from the catchment hash (`seed ^ 0x4859_4452_4F4C_4F47`, signed catchment X/Z). Its origin/rotation and marine ellipse are the existing catchment's. No spawn call or decorated-terrain recursion is used to construct a feature.
- Local U follows the catchment's marine axis, V runs along the western shoreline; +Y is up. World columns are sampled at X/Z cell centers in `f64`. Catchment/root products and chunk halos use `i64`, with checked conversion to block coordinates. World Y remains `-64..80` (top exclusive).
- The segment center is local V `146 + hash variation in -4..4`; its half-length is `76..84`. The complete blend/occupancy envelope is V `center ± (half-length + 12)`, U `232..512`. This includes the inland apron, weathering, stack bases, and arch. Existing 760-block catchment reach covers it after rotation/128-block inland translation; the hydrology query's four-block halo still covers raw shoreline smoothing. Tree-canopy reach remains two blocks, and coastal destinations reject tree decoration.
- The coastal edge follows the marine ellipse, with a bounded three-block headland undulation. Placement is on the positive-V side away from the estuary/river mouths. Actual freshwater occupancy always takes precedence, and existing lakes/drainage outside the envelope are unchanged.
- Validated M1-02 refinement: freshwater exclusion includes each lake's complete existing bank apron (64 blocks for primary sources, 32 for nearby lakes), then blends over another 20 blocks. Diagnostic viewpoint candidates use bounded actual line-of-sight checks to at least three stacks; feet clearance alone did not prevent a near ledge hiding the view.
- Seed chooses five or six main stacks, irregular spacing, footprint aspect/rotation, capped 18–34-block height above the mean sea, layering phase, cliff height, cove placement, and arch proportions. Feature order never depends on chunk order.

## Column/voxel/water precedence

- `HydrologyRegion` prepares catchment/coastal roots once per query region. A small copyable coastal column projection is carried in the existing hydrology profile; per-voxel work never reconstructs roots.
- Apply the coastal profile **after** the original bounded hydrology bed filter. The original filter therefore cannot round away stacks/arch supports or feed tall crowns into neighboring water-bed calculations. Out-of-envelope profiles are byte-for-byte unchanged.
- `Column.height` continues to mean the ground/seabed cap before cave carving, **not** the tallest overhanging rock. Cliff/stack/support columns are ordinary solid caps. At an arch opening the cap remains the actual bed/beach, and an explicit inclusive `[first_y, last_y]` limestone crown span supplies roof occupancy above open air. No disconnected water-span model is needed: the entire first crown starts above mean Y=18 water.
- Shared `hydrology_block` handles coastal rock/layering/roof before air/water; the existing bedrock rule has highest precedence. Coastal fill/seals suppress unrelated caves from the designed rock, and coastal roots/destinations suppress trees. Normal player edits apply afterward and can mine any ordinary coastal rock.
- Water is created only over supported beds below its mean surface. Solid stack/support columns have no water profile; opening columns retain `Ocean`, salinity 1, the catchment's finite marine current, and their actual seabed bottom. `Terrain::water_at` and resident `Chunk::water` must both agree with actual `Water` voxels. Visual waves do not modify occupancy or trigger spreading after mining.
- Direct blocks, full columns, bounded batches, dense chunks, procedural fallback, and mesh halos all use the same profile/projection/material function. Existing IDs remain append-only and atlas IDs remain separate; no new IDs are needed for this milestone.

## Arch and atmosphere

- The arch is a finite coastal ridge with two grounded supports, a contiguous crown, and a through-opening along U. Opening width along V is at least four blocks, height at least three; its lower air interval is above mean water. Supports/crown use editable limestone and thin sediment/calcite layers.
- `Game::environment` must classify actual open-air roof geometry, not crown height. A bounded local lateral/open-sky survey, cached by camera cell and edit revision, distinguishes exposed under-arch air from enclosed caves. Vertical mesh skylight still shades the underside; outdoor atmosphere does not become black cave fog merely because a roof exists.
- M1-03 implementation: explicit generated roof intervals are resident chunk metadata; actual voxels/edits remain authoritative. Four cardinal eight-block sky-spill corridors distinguish the short open arch from enclosure. Camera exposure caches invalidate on edit revision. CPU meshes share the same bounded field across seams, with up to four-block vertical spill to shade rounded undersides (0.32–0.48 ambient). Generated roof bounds mark only exposed opaque faces for outdoor fog; cave faces outside the field keep existing darkness. Work is projected/cached on the worker, never generated in a shader/render callback. The existing 3×3 edit halo includes this reach.
- M1-05 review hardening: camera overhang sampling uses **resident columns only** and includes the surrounding 3×3 chunk revisions in its cell cache. Missing/resumed residency invalidates the hint; the render-frame environment path no longer constructs a new coastal halo. Lower-sky darkening additionally requires real nearby geological side-wall enclosure, so a broad outdoor roof does not create a black marine horizon. Both roof and air-side opaque faces receive the exposed-overhang atmosphere flag when measured spill is positive.
- M1-03 visual refinement: stack roots move to 36–47 blocks offshore to keep their components detached from the arch. A seven-block dry beach ribbon joins the cove to an actual supported arch shore view. Opening beds are sealed at no higher than Y=14; crowns remain above water. The lower-sky fallback now checks actual enclosed air along the view, rather than darkening every ground-level sea horizon.
- Preserve stationary edited-roof invalidation, dark natural caves, exposed pits, foliage, headlamp, underwater transitions, and phasing. Regression tests cover both procedural and resident/edit-aware exposure.

## Approach and diagnostics

- Existing dry spawn selection remains the normal entry point and never asks for a coast or route. The early site is the catchment at `(0,0)`; selection is a bounded deterministic readout of real generated ground, not a teleport or forced clearing.
- A broad inland blend and cove ramp are natural parts of the coastal shape. Navigation uses rock exposure, headlands, shoreline, and skyline only; there is no new HUD direction, marker, map, or home shortcut.
- A CPU-only coast probe surveys at most a 640×640-block surface region, caching actual full columns/resident chunks. Route nodes require supported feet and full standing-body width/height; edges check swept clearance, rises/jumps, and reversible drops. Replay approach/cove critical sections through `Player::update_with_water`, including reverse travel, without flight, building, mining, swimming, or R.
- Route implementation budget: at most 1,681 (41×41) transient diagnostic chunks, 100,000 expanded surface nodes, and 4,096 points per leg. A* is confined to the spawn/view/cove/arch-view rectangle plus a 48-block margin (each side at most 640). Cardinal one-block edges have continuous full-body clearance and at most one-block reversible rises/drops, with jump headroom. Replay all accepted legs forward/reverse with the real controller, bounded steps and explicit failure on blocked, unsupported, submerged, or embedded outcomes. This CPU diagnostic is never called from interactive rendering.
- Initial acceptance seeds: default, 42, 0, 1, 7, 2026; additionally sample other seeds for failures. Diagnostic coordinates/routes are not gameplay navigation. Failed routes are failures, not repaired by placing a diagnostic camera.
- Private bounded GPU scenes: land vista, cove, grounded stacks/seabed, arch aperture/underside, underwater marine optics, plus edit/remesh/reload and isolated save round trip. Inspect at default radius and check radii 2/12 gracefully; do not promise visibility beyond resident fog.

## Versions, limits, acceptance

- Intentionally increment saved generation version from **1 to 2** for coastal occupancy. File format remains 1. Incompatible saves remain occupied and show the M1-00 actionable notice; no migration/reset or real-slot test is authorized.
- Update goldens only for measured changed coastal fixtures; retain independent unmodified controls, seam/metadata checks, negative/extreme-coordinate cases, and deterministic order tests.
- Residency/worker policies remain unchanged. No coast search on the render thread; feature projection is bounded per existing generation region. Compare fresh generation/mesh/geometry and GPU frame samples in the same build mode/hardware with the pre-coast baseline.
- M1-05 cannot be marked owner-accepted by CPU/controller/GPU evidence. A hands-on approximately 30-minute fresh-world walking expedition, return, save/quit, and Continue is required before M2 begins.
